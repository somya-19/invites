Questions

Anything else? Just message us.

Will the characters really look like us?

They're made from your photos as stylised 3D characters. You see a still preview first and we only animate after you approve it.

How do I pay?

50% advance by UPI to start, and the balance when your video is ready.

Is the music copyright-free?

Yes. It's original music, safe to share on WhatsApp, Instagram and YouTube.

Can the text be in Hindi or another language?

Yes. Send us the text in any language and we'll use it.

Which occasions do you make invites for?

Any celebration: weddings, engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, griha pravesh, baby showers, naming ceremonies, festival parties and office events. We also make surprise videos, like anniversary gifts or proposals. If you can invite people to it, we can animate it.

Can kids or the whole family be in the video?

Yes. Send clear photos of everyone you want in it: the birthday child, grandparents, the whole family. Bigger groups may need the Full Invite package.

We live outside India. Can we order?

Of course. Everything happens online. You can also order through our Fiverr gig.
